- [ ] The article contains a distinct first-hand or reusable element.
- [ ] Sensitive-topic boundaries are visible and specific.
- [ ] The title and description accurately describe the page.
- [ ] The author and dates are visible and supplied to schema.
- [ ] Internal links help the reader progress through the cluster.
- [ ] The CTA matches the reader’s stage and the actual offer.

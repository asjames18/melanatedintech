# Product Specification: Ministry & Nonprofit AI Operations Kit

- **New slug:** `ministry-nonprofit-ai-operations-kit`
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Price:** $59 one-time
- **Delivery:** One Markdown governance and pilot workbook rendered after purchase and downloadable as `.md`
- **Primary buyer:** Church or nonprofit leader preparing a responsible, low-risk AI-supported workflow
- **Authority cluster:** AI for Churches and Nonprofits

## Product promise

Help a church or nonprofit document responsible AI use before connecting confidential data or live systems: stewardship charter, use-case boundaries, data inventory, vendor review, acceptable-use policy, human-approval matrix, pilot tests, incident response, and leadership review.

## Explicit exclusions

- No legal, privacy, security, theological, pastoral, financial, safeguarding, employment, or regulatory advice
- No compliance certification
- No hosted software, provider account, integration, deployment, or managed implementation
- No authorization to process confidential member, donor, client, beneficiary, child, counseling, prayer, giving, health, or safeguarding information

## License

Single-organization internal-use license. Adaptation for the buyer's ministry or nonprofit is allowed; resale and redistribution are not.

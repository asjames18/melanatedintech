# Brand Assets and Fork Rebranding Guide

The Melanated In Tech source code is available under the MIT License. The brand assets listed below are **not** granted for use as the identity, logo, favicon, marketing artwork, domain, or public presentation of an independent fork or derivative.

## Reserved brand assets

The following are reserved Melanated In Tech brand assets:

| Asset or surface | Required action for a public fork or deployment |
|---|---|
| `public/brand/**` | Remove or replace all Melanated In Tech logo and brand image files. |
| `public/favicon.ico`, `public/favicon.svg`, `public/favicon-32.png` | Replace with a distinct favicon and application icon. |
| `public/og/**` and other social-preview artwork carrying Melanated In Tech identity | Remove or replace with independently created, non-confusing artwork. |
| Site header, footer, page title, metadata, email sender name, and legal notices | Replace the Melanated In Tech name, logo, contact details, and official links with your own. |
| `melanatedintech.com` and related official domains, accounts, and handles | Do not use, imitate, or imply control of them. Configure your own domain, support address, social accounts, analytics, and deployment settings. |

## Rebranding checklist for forks

Before you host, distribute, market, or make a fork available to users, complete this checklist:

1. Select a distinct project and organization name.
2. Replace all logo files, icons, favicons, and social-preview images.
3. Update the visible site name, metadata, legal pages, footer, email sender identity, support contact, and links.
4. Configure your own domain, authentication, database, payment, email, analytics, and cloud credentials.
5. Remove all sample content, prices, customer references, case material, and operational claims that do not belong to your independent project.
6. Add a factual attribution notice if desired, making clear that the fork is independently maintained and not endorsed by Melanated In Tech.

See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) for the complete trademark and fork-use policy, and [NOTICE](NOTICE) for the code-versus-brand licensing boundary.

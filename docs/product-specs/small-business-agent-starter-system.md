# Product Specification: Small Business Agent Starter System

- **New slug:** `small-business-agent-starter-system`
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Price:** $59 one-time
- **Delivery:** One Markdown implementation workbook rendered after purchase and downloadable as `.md`
- **Primary buyer:** Owner or operations lead choosing and testing a first small-business AI agent workflow
- **Authority cluster:** AI Agents for Small Business

## Product promise

Help a small business select one appropriate workflow and produce the documentation required for a disconnected or controlled pilot: workflow brief, process map, data and action boundaries, output contract, approval rules, test cases, cost plan, launch decision, and 30-day operating review.

## Explicit exclusions

- No hosted agent or software account
- No integration, credential setup, or deployment
- No provider/API credits
- No custom consulting or guaranteed time/revenue result
- No legal, financial, employment, privacy, security, or regulatory advice

Single-organization internal-use license. Adaptation for the buyer's own workflows is allowed; resale and redistribution are not.

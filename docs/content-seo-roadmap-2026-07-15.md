# Melanated In Tech Content and SEO Execution Roadmap

**Start date:** July 15, 2026  
**North star:** Build a trusted, faceless AI education and digital-product company whose useful content compounds through search, email, and products.

## Strategic focus

The site already has enough breadth. The next stage is depth, accuracy, proof, and conversion. New publishing should support four authority clusters instead of adding disconnected posts.

1. **AI Agents for Beginners** — explain agents clearly and help a reader choose and test a first workflow.
2. **AI Agents for Small Business** — show practical, low-risk workflows with honest cost and ROI frameworks.
3. **AI Safety and Evaluation** — give small teams usable guardrails, evaluation sets, logging, and approval patterns.
4. **AI for Churches and Nonprofits** — become the practical, stewardship-first resource for mission-driven organizations.

Each cluster must connect an open pillar guide, supporting articles, a free resource, a learning path, a relevant agent, and one substantial paid product.

## Quality gates

### Every indexable article

- Targets one primary search intent.
- Opens with a direct, self-contained answer.
- Shows a named author, published date, and reviewed/updated date.
- Links factual and technical claims to current primary sources.
- Includes a worked example, reusable template, table, diagram, or test result.
- Labels suggested thresholds and illustrative numbers as examples.
- States limitations for legal, financial, hiring, privacy, compliance, or ministry topics.
- Links to three or more relevant internal resources without forcing exact-match anchors.
- Has accurate title, description, canonical, Article schema, `datePublished`, and `dateModified`.
- Has a documented review interval: 30–60 days for model/tool content and 6–12 months for durable guidance.

### Every indexable product page

- Says exactly what the buyer receives and in which file format.
- Lists audience, outcome, contents, prerequisites, setup time, version, license, and support boundary.
- Includes a useful preview or sample.
- Avoids claims of integrations or automation that are not included.
- Uses Product schema only when price, availability, and delivery claims are accurate.

### Every agent page

- Separates what works on the site now from what requires external setup.
- States that connected tools, permissions, deployment, and external accounts are not included unless explicitly listed.
- Includes human-review boundaries for sensitive use cases.
- Avoids guaranteed rankings, revenue, funding, fairness, compliance, or professional correctness.

### Every niche SEO page

- Contains a niche-specific workflow, inputs, worked output, risks, FAQs, and relevant tool recommendations.
- Is meaningfully different from other niche pages beyond replacing the niche name.
- Links into one authority cluster and one matching lead magnet.
- Remains out of the sitemap or noindexed until it meets the gate.

## Twelve-week roadmap

### Weeks 1–2: Trust and crawl cleanup

- Noindex internal search and remove it from the sitemap.
- Remove thin community threads from the sitemap until they meet a quality threshold.
- Add explicit Markdown delivery labels to product pages and purchase panels.
- Add “works now / requires setup / boundaries” content to agent pages.
- Correct product-count contradictions and inaccurate PDF/package/file claims.
- Replace “fine-tune” language in GPT Trainer with accurate prompt-configuration language.
- Rename the Proof page or add real evidence.
- Archive completed challenges and expose a clear current/completed state.

**Exit gate:** Search engines are directed only toward useful public pages, and no prominent offer promises a format or capability it does not deliver.

### Weeks 3–4: Freshness and authority signals

- Rewrite or temporarily unpublish the five obsolete technical articles.
- Correct unsupported financial math and universal thresholds.
- Add authors to the seven unattributed articles.
- Add visible dates, review dates, primary-source links, and `dateModified` schema.
- Recalculate all read-time values.
- Create a quarterly content-review queue and a faster tool/model review queue.

**Exit gate:** All high-priority technical and money-related pages are current, sourced, attributable, and dated.

### Weeks 5–6: Consolidation and internal linking

- Merge the eight overlapping article pairs identified in the audit.
- Add permanent redirects from retired URLs.
- Build cluster navigation and contextual links across articles, agents, tools, paths, and products.
- Correct every learning-path action so it matches the actual UI and delivery format.
- Give community assignments a self-review rubric.

**Exit gate:** One primary URL owns each search intent, no learning step asks for a nonexistent action, and every priority page participates in a cluster.

### Weeks 7–9: Flagship products

Build and validate three substantial offers:

1. **Small Business Agent Starter System**
2. **Ministry and Nonprofit AI Operations Kit**
3. **AI Agent Evaluation and Safety Kit**

Each offer should include editable source files, implementation instructions, worked examples, checklists, versioning, license terms, and an open sample. Hide, reprice, or mark other thin premium products as coming soon until they pass the product quality gate.

**Exit gate:** Each promoted product is worth its stated price and its page accurately previews the delivered artifact.

### Weeks 10–12: Authority clusters and distribution

- Publish or refresh the four pillar guides.
- Deepen the first five niche pages: churches, nonprofits, real-estate agents, hair salons, and HVAC contractors.
- Create one free lead magnet per pillar.
- Launch one weekly email that summarizes and links to the strongest knowledge asset.
- Repurpose each pillar update into text posts and carousels without creating disconnected content.
- Begin monthly manual monitoring for target queries across Google, ChatGPT search, and Perplexity.

**Exit gate:** Four complete topic clusters connect search discovery to email capture and honest product offers.

## Publishing rhythm after cleanup

### Weekly

- One substantive pillar or cluster update.
- One newsletter built from that knowledge asset.
- Three to five text/social adaptations.
- One refresh of an older technical page.
- One product or lead-magnet improvement.

### Monthly

- Review search queries, click-through rate, email conversion, and product-page behavior.
- Test 20 priority questions in Google, ChatGPT search, and Perplexity; record who is cited.
- Refresh model/tool pages and remove stale claims.
- Select the next month’s content from search data, audience questions, and product gaps.

## Automation design

Automation should prepare work, not publish unverified technical claims.

1. **Research agent:** collects current primary-source updates and candidate queries.
2. **Content strategist:** assigns the topic to a pillar and defines search intent, reader outcome, and CTA.
3. **Drafting agent:** creates the answer block, outline, article draft, FAQ candidates, and social adaptations.
4. **Evidence checker:** verifies links, dates, model names, prices, and numerical claims against primary sources.
5. **SEO checker:** checks title, description, canonical, headings, internal links, schema inputs, and cannibalization.
6. **Human approval:** approves sensitive, commercial, legal, financial, hiring, ministry, or controversial content.
7. **Publisher:** schedules only approved material.
8. **Refresh monitor:** flags pages when sources change, links break, or the review interval expires.

The first version should create a daily review packet, not auto-publish. The packet should include the chosen topic, sources, confidence notes, article outline, direct answer, suggested internal links, CTA, and repurposed posts.

## Decision rules

- Do not publish a new page when improving an existing page would satisfy the same intent.
- Do not index a page simply because it can be generated.
- Do not publish a number without a source, calculation, or explicit “example” label.
- Do not sell an integration unless the integration is included and tested.
- Do not let automation approve its own sensitive claims.
- Do not scale promotion until fulfillment quality and tracking are verified.

# AI Training Pricing Research — August 27, 2026

## Sources reviewed

1. **PriorAIty — AI Discovery Session**  
   URL: https://www.prioraity.io/consulting/discovery-session  
   This provider lists a 60–90 minute leadership-team AI discovery session at **$250–$500**. The session covers current state, challenges, existing AI usage, opportunities, initial recommendations, and includes a written discovery summary within three business days. The provider expressly positions it as an honest determination of whether a deeper engagement is appropriate.

2. **30Elevate — AI Training for Business (April 2026, updated July 2026)**  
   URL: https://30elevate.com/en/blog/ai-training-for-business/  
   This provider describes its own 4–8 hour, up-to-12-person team workshops at **$1,800–$5,600**, varying by scope and industry tailoring. It distinguishes owner-focused consultation/strategy from team training, and cautions against generic or unsupported AI training promises.

3. **U.S. Chamber of Commerce — Comprehensive Guide to AI Training for Small Businesses (March 2026)**  
   URL: https://www.uschamber.com/co/start/strategy/ai-training-guide-small-business  
   The guide identifies substantial small-business demand for additional AI implementation training and highlights that free generic resources exist. It implies a paid provider needs to compete on practical, situation-specific help rather than generic introductory content alone.

## Review use

These sources are contextual market signals, not a pricing guarantee or a basis for claiming average market rates. They support the conclusion that Melanated In Tech’s $297 90-minute AI Clarity Session is a plausible introductory price if its scope is tightly bounded: a short intake, a 90-minute session, a prompt pack, and a concise next-step summary; team workshops and implementation must be priced separately.

## Direct answer

An AI agent is software that uses an AI model to decide which steps and tools to use toward a goal, observes the results, and continues within defined limits. Unlike a basic chatbot, an agent can manage part of a workflow—but people still need to control permissions, review sensitive actions, and measure results.

## Sensitive-use boundaries

- Do not imply autonomous operation is inherently desirable.
- Require human approval for money, records, external messages, legal/financial/hiring/compliance decisions, and sensitive personal data.
- Avoid universal thresholds; organizations choose limits based on risk and policy.
- Do not promise productivity or ROI outcomes without measured evidence.

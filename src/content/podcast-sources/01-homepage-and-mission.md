## 🎯 SECTION 1: Core Platform Identity

### Mission Statement
> *"Help people confidently understand, build, deploy, and benefit from AI Agents."*

**Melanated in Tech** is a practical AI education platform, interactive tools hub, operating library, marketplace, and professional services destination. It is **NOT an AI news website**—it is built for long-term authority, practical application, and empowering individuals and teams to build and benefit from AI agentic workflows.

---

## 👥 SECTION 2: Target Audience & Who We Serve

Melanated in Tech is designed for four primary groups:
1. **Beginners Learning AI:** People looking for clear, non-intimidating, plain-English guidance without jargon or technical gatekeeping.
2. **Small Businesses & Entrepreneurs:** Business owners seeking to automate workflows, reduce overhead, and scale operations using AI agents.
3. **Churches, Ministries & Nonprofits:** Mission-driven organizations looking to streamline volunteer coordination, sermon adaptation, and community outreach.
4. **Technology Professionals & Developers:** Builders needing production-grade tools, Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, system prompt composers, and evaluation benchmarks.

### 1. Knowledge Hub
Practical AI agent playbooks covering 4 critical tracks:
- **Build:** First agent loops, prompt engineering, RAG pipelines, and MCP connectors.
- **Operate:** Golden-set evaluation, logging, cost optimization, and review rhythms.
- **Secure:** Guardrails, prompt injection safety, and human-in-the-loop approval gates.
- **Decide:** Business ROI calculators, build vs. buy frameworks, and vendor evaluation.

### 2. Interactive Tools Hub (12 Free Tools)
Hands-on web apps that allow users to generate system prompts, build SOPs, estimate token costs, design multi-agent workflows, and create MCP servers without writing code from scratch.

### 3. Agent Marketplace & Products
Curated, battle-tested AI agent blueprints, prompt libraries, and automation packs that solve specific business problems.

### 4. Guided Learning Paths
Structured journeys leading learners step-by-step from beginner concepts to deploying autonomous agentic systems.

### 5. Professional Services
Direct partnership with the Melanated in Tech team for 90-minute strategy sprints, custom AI agent builds, team workshops, and ministry AI implementations.

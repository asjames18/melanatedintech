**Effective date:** August 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Melanated In Tech name and visual identity may be used in connection with this repository and its forks.

**Melanated In Tech**, the Melanated In Tech logos, the Melanated In Tech domain names, and the distinctive visual identity used for the official website and services are referred to in this policy as the **Marks**. The Marks identify the official Melanated In Tech project, products, services, and community.

The MIT License in this repository grants permission to use the covered software code. It does **not** grant permission to use the Marks as the name, identity, domain, logo, or endorsement for another product, service, website, organization, or deployment.

## 2. Permitted factual use

You may use the Melanated In Tech name in a truthful, non-misleading way to:

- Identify this repository as the original source of a fork or derivative.
- State that your work is “based on,” “forked from,” or “compatible with” the Melanated In Tech codebase.
- Link to the official repository or website.

This factual use must not be more prominent than your own project name and must not suggest sponsorship, certification, partnership, endorsement, or control by Melanated In Tech.

## 3. Required rebranding for forks and derivatives

If you distribute, deploy, host, market, or offer a fork or derivative of this repository, you must do all of the following before presenting it to users or customers:

1. **Choose and use a distinct product name.** Do not name your product, company, service, repository, package, or deployment “Melanated In Tech” or use a confusingly similar name.
2. **Replace the visual identity.** Remove or replace the Melanated In Tech logos, favicons, Open Graph images, branded artwork, color treatments, and other brand assets in `public/brand/`, favicon files, and related marketing surfaces.
3. **Replace official identifiers.** Change page titles, metadata, footer notices, contact addresses, social handles, email sender names, domain references, analytics identifiers, and support links that identify the official Melanated In Tech service.
4. **Do not imply affiliation.** Do not state or imply that your fork is the official Melanated In Tech website, an authorized deployment, a certified implementation, a partner service, or endorsed by Melanated In Tech.
5. **Do not use official domains or accounts.** Do not deploy your fork to a Melanated In Tech domain, create a confusingly similar domain or social handle, or use official Melanated In Tech credentials, API keys, accounts, payment configurations, or customer data.
6. **Keep attribution accurate.** You may include a concise notice such as: “This project is a fork of code originally published by Melanated In Tech. It is independently maintained and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Melanated In Tech.”

## 4. Prohibited uses

Without prior written permission, you may not:

- Use the Marks as the primary name, logo, favicon, social handle, domain, or marketing identity of a fork or derivative.
- Copy the official website’s brand presentation in a way likely to confuse users about source, sponsorship, or affiliation.
- Use the Marks in connection with products or services that are unlawful, deceptive, unsafe, or materially inconsistent with the official project’s stated boundaries.
- Register, seek to register, or claim rights in a name, logo, domain, or account that is identical or confusingly similar to the Marks.

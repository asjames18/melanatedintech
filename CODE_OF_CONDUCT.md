# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Melanated In Tech is a welcoming, constructive space for people with different backgrounds, experiences, roles, and levels of technical confidence. We are committed to participation that is respectful, useful, and grounded in care for the people affected by technology.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Assume good intent while discussing ideas directly and respectfully.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people who are new to open source or do not write code professionally.
- Credit the work and lived expertise of others.
- Focus feedback on the work, evidence, and project scope—not on the worth of another person.
- Protect privacy and never share personal, client, or confidential information without authorization.
- Respect project boundaries, including limits on claims about safety, compliance, and automation.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, hateful language, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive conduct intended to intimidate newcomers or silence a viewpoint.
- Publishing private information, credentials, customer data, or confidential material.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, ignoring documented scope, or making bad-faith demands.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness a concern, please report it privately to **hello@melanatedintech.com** with the subject line `Melanated In Tech Code of Conduct`. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

The project steward will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible. Responses may include clarification, a warning, content removal, a temporary restriction, or removal from project spaces depending on the situation. Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issue trackers, discussions, working sessions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
